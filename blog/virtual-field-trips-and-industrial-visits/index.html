Virtual Field Trips: Save Time And Money With Inexpensive Virtual Industrial Visits

  • Article By:

    Eva Jones

  • Published On:

    02 September, 2022

  • Updated On:

    20 December 2023

Field trips are among the most exciting parts of higher education regardless of engineering, science grads, or any other stream.

Field trips allow students to gain practical learning experience in the real world and find relevance to what they are learning at institutions.

The higher education sector worldwide will escalate to $169.72 Billion by 2028. (Fortune Business Insights)

Higher education is gradually gaining colossal potential, offering promising prospects. 

Suppose we circle only around the Indian higher education sector, approx. one hundred fifty million people belong to the age group of 18-23 years. (InsideIIM)

Hence, the higher education sector needs to become robust enough to accommodate all these students with quality education.

The educational field trip is an essential component of quality higher education. Due to the surge in technological adoption of VR in higher education, virtual field trips are gradually replacing regular field trips.

And the efficiency gets doubled while arranging industrial visits for engineering and science grad students.

Interactive learning and online resources have already exposed students to a great scope to gain real-time learning experiences. 

Likewise, Virtual field trips, especially for industrial visits, offer students opportunities to have real working environment experiences at their fingertips.

What Are Virtual Field Trips For Industrial Visits?


Virtual field trips are explorations of places through the internet and immersive technologies.

Professors can present VR tours on various syllabus themes to offer students a structured learning experience.

Virtual field trips are an interactive and dynamic form of learning that is freely available, making access no longer a concern. 

The pre-screened and thematically organized information enables science grad, post-grad, and engineering students to bridge the gap between their classroom-based learning and real-world work environment.

The virtual tour market will reach 6.5 billion by 2030. (PR News Wire). The growing demand for industrial visits has its part in the growth.

Industrial visit is a crucial part of the academic curriculum for graduate and post-graduate courses. Industrial visits fill the gap between theoretical learning and practical exposure for students.

Students get first-hand experience around industries, evaluating the input and output of various business operations and processes.

If educational institutions intend to offer their students learning experiences beyond the classroom walls, industrial tours allow them to provide a holistic learning approach.


For instance, manufacturing industry tours let students learn about the present market trends, the latest technological trends, the evolution of business processes, and the future industry scenario.

Virtual industry visits have modified conventional industry tours.

Through virtual 3D simulation, educational institutes can provide their students with industrial tours staying within the classroom environment.

Colleges and universities do not need to arrange trips for students. Students can acquire the same learning experience while staying within the comfort of their classroom.

The 360-degree view of machines, equipment, and other business processes enable students to enhance their learning outcome.

Learning from textbooks and lectures does not ensure holistic learning. Hands-on learning is essential to understand work processes and business functions better.

Virtual Industry visit merges theoretical and practical learning, bringing concepts to action but virtually, within a safe environment.

Benefits of Virtual Industry Visits


Virtual field trips, specifically industrial visits, help students to maximize their technical and management skills. Being inexpensive, VR industrial trips enable educational institutes to save time, and money, followed by multiple benefits:

✔️ Interactive Learning Experience From Industry Experts


Whether engineering, IT, or management students, all of them require industry-specific knowledge and guideline to enhance their learning outcomes. 

For example, engineering students seek hands-on experience in automobile manufacturing, vehicle engines, manufacturing processes, and other equipment functions.

On the other hand, management students require a deep insight into the present marketing trends to upgrade their knowledge about the industry.

VR industrial visits aid students in meeting market expectations.

Industry leaders and professionals can influence students by sharing their industry-specific experience and knowledge.

It allows students to understand precisely how the actual industry works or what kind of management skills and leadership qualities they need to acquire.

The interactive and engaging virtual environment equips students with the right skills through hands-on training on the latest technologies and trends in the industry.

✔️ Learning Industry-Specific Management Skills


Virtual field trips around industries or a specific industry show students how professionals perform their work responsibilities like benchmarking, quality analysis, production, Etc.

Students learn how industrialists and their managers handle thousands of workers, maintaining quality norms, environment compliances, Etc.

They get a broad perspective on how professionals like workers, engineers, product designers, production managers, Etc., work in harmony to meet the organization's goal.

✔️ Improving Job Prospects


Students can leverage the opportunities of virtual industrial visits to ensure quality and sustainable relationships with the companies.

It can help them in their future job hunting.

They will become able to contact these organizations for training and internships.

Moreover, VR industrial trips establish positive interaction between the students and the HR managers of the organization. It can help them in their future job prospects overall.

✔️ Experiential Learning Blends With Practical Learning


Whether K-12 students or engineering students, practical learning is crucial for all. The regular conventional lecture sessions bring monotony to students.

Virtual field trips are the most engaging way to boost students' learning abilities, putting all the necessary information into their brains. Students get the chance to learn specific topics beyond the classroom environment.

The virtual industrial visits bring unique opportunities for students to experience real machines, workstations, systems, plants, assembly lines, etc., within the safe virtual environment.

They can engage their brains in practical learning, staying within a safe virtual environment.


From production to operational and management policies, everything becomes clear to students. VR industrial trips allow students to experience free conversations with industry leaders and faculty members.

Wrapping Up


From industry-specific environment analysis to boosting industry attractiveness, VR industry tours cover all.

Whether it is about arranging virtual field trips for engineering or management students, it saves time and money for educational institutions.

In fact, VR field trips magnify students' experiential learning. It complements traditional industry tours, filling the learning gaps.
