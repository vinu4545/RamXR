4 Creative Ways to Teach Engineering Through Modern Tech

  • Article By:

    Eva Jones

  • Published On:

    14 September, 2022

  • Updated On:

    17 April 2024

Engineering is one of the popular graduation courses. From various engineering courses such as mechanical, electrical, civil, electronics, computer, etc., students choose courses with their interests and opportunities.

  • According to National Science Foundation, there are approximately 7.5 million engineers and science graduates in the world.

Engineering education is based on many practical advantages and uses. For example, if there is training for mechanical engineering, then unless students learn subjects practically, they won't be industry ready. 

Students need to understand theory to score well and excel in practicals to stand strong in the professional world. Moreover, to inhale the latest technologies, one needs to have a robust technological base. Because when the foundation is strong, the development is stronger.

To make this development more robust, appropriately teaching engineering is vital. You can make use of various development strategies to let students succeed holistically. You can follow the latest educational technology trends. In addition, using modern technology can be the best way.

In this blog, you will read about some creative ways to include technology in engineering training.

Let us start.

AI-based learning can be effective


Artificial intelligence can help integrate various parts of the system. Some of its offerings, such as personalization, can be significantly effective in engineering platforms for personalized content suggestions. In addition, AI can foster motivation.

Moreover, its benefits in digital assistance for engineering education can be phenomenal. Educators can use AI-based platforms to manage administrative tasks, personalized academic content curation, and attractive AI features. For example, a teacher can not be available 24*7, but AI can be! AI-based chatbots can help solve queries when professors are not available.

You can make use of activity-based learning


Activity-based learning is one of the effective ways of learning. For example, many companies make use of tasks such as escape rooms as a team-building activity. In such a way, modern technology-based activities can help engineering students understand concepts easily.

Activities in engineering education can include individual or team projects, industrial tours, site visits, etc. Modern technologies can help creatively achieve these activities while not being present in that place.

How?

For example, metaverse, virtual reality, or mixed reality supports the gaming environment. With the help of these technology-based activities, students can learn about complex concepts in an engaging way. 

Have a look at this oil refinery tour as a great example of it. Such virtual tours can be powerful in engineering studies.


AR/VR can be game changers in distance engineering learning


Augmentation is not a new concept, but augmented reality is! With AR, the virtual world is brought on an electronic device. And with virtual reality, a user can get completely involved in a virtual environment.

The immersive feature of virtual reality is effective in teaching engineering students of various streams. For example, check out this 2D representation of a bridge construction site. It will let students feel like they are present at the virtual site.


In addition, the explanation in a guided mode will give students details about every part and process of the bridge construction. Furthermore, such virtual and augmented reality-based activities in engineering studies will enhance the learning quality of students.

Metaverse brings various opportunities in engineering education


After the boom of virtual reality, its extension- the metaverse is taking all over the world! Metaverse is still in the research and implementation phase. Yet, many experts are looking forward to its growth in education.

  • The metaverse market in education is said to be USD 94.9 billion by 2030. (Globe-News Wire)

Metaverse can give a creative touch of avatars to students' appearances. It will be a great world for engineering teammates to come together for various activities and tasks. Moreover, engineering education in the metaverse will have access to robust technological growth. It can develop the future of future engineers.

Let us sum it up!

Technologies can provide creative solutions to a crisis such as a pandemic. In such times, when the world was shut down, development like e-learning kept education systems alive. Various technologies and tools in education got significance.

In this blog, we have told you about four creative uses of modern technologies and tools in engineering education. These technologies can enhance the values of engineering education. Connect with iXR Labs to understand the power of augmented reality and virtual reality in education.
