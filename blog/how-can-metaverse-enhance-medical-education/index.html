How Can Metaverse Enhance Medical Education?

  • Article By:

    Eva Jones

  • Published On:

    09 August, 2022

  • Updated On:

    09 July 2024

The covid pandemic made us realize the value of remote education. All the classrooms were shifted online. Moreover, many technologies like VR and the metaverse in education platform received acknowledgment. However, there were many drawbacks of such online learning.

Student engagement was low.

Students and even teachers felt disconnected. It increased isolation.

Having control of a class was a big task for professors as well.

These challenges specified the need for an alternative learning solution. And here is the metaverse! Metaverse tackles many of the challenges mentioned above. It increases engagement. In addition, it promotes the active participation of students. Furthermore, it offers a platform that is based on virtual reality.

The metaverse has many advantages to various education streams. This blog will tell you about the significance of metaverse to medical education.

Medical Education and Technologies In It

Medical education is the need of an hour. The requirements of current society are not just an education but learning that is rigorous, deep, and practical oriented. No matter how much technical advancement takes place every day or minute, if it is not able to help the healthcare system, it is not significant.

However, almost every technological revolution contributes to the healthcare domain somehow. A great example is a medical education. It is one of the revolutionizing areas.

Thanks to the technologies and research in the medical field that have enhanced people's lives. Earlier, an individual used to have a lifespan of just between 20-30 years. Then, medical advancements advancement have shown the magic to all of us.

While speaking about the technologies boosting medical education, we can mention a few technologies such as virtual reality, augmented reality, 3D technology, artificial intelligence, and now metaverse.

Virtual reality and augmented reality have significant impacts on education and other healthcare areas. In addition, artificial intelligence is also a part of enhancing medical training. Moreover, the 3D invention makes medical learning more interesting and clear.

Now let us move towards the significance of metaverse in medical education. 

What is in the Metaverse?

By the year 2030, the worldwide metaverse market is said to have a value of USD 678.80 billion, according to the report by Strategic Market Research.

Metaverse is a promising technology. Moreover, it can have a massive impact on education. The metaverse consists of a 3D immersive world where users can appear in distinct avatars. Furthermore, users will be able to interact and have social connections.

Metaverse combines various technologies. It takes the best from all to give a unique and advanced platform. 

Can metaverse be valuable in medical education?

A report by Vantage Market Research says that in the year 2021, the multinational metaverse in the education had a value of USD 4.39 billion. Further, the year 2028 may witness its reach of USD  32.39 billion.

InsightAce Analytic says that the metaverse in the healthcare market was USD 5.06 billion across the globe in 2021. In addition, it may reach USD 71.97 billion by 2030.

Medical education can have many advantages from the metaverse. First, it can create an immersive and recreational learning experience. Moreover, they will be tailormade for students. The following are some of the benefits of the metaverse to medical education.

Virtual Yet Realistic Learning

Virtual reality is the essence of the metaverse. Therefore, it will offer the foremost benefit of virtual reality applications in medical education. With metaverse, you can have a walk the 3D model of a human body. In addition, you will be able to discuss the diagnostic methods with peers.

Collaboration and Peer Interaction Will Have Much Significance.

A doctor, or say, surgeon, may have excellence in his domain. But he can not operate on a patient individually. He will need help from many fellow doctors, maybe to check the blood samples, anesthesia, or many other tasks.

Therefore, this field requires a lot of professional communication ethics. With the feature of social interaction in the metaverse, it can be easy and effortless.

For example, while learning a particular subject or experimenting on a virtual model in the metaverse, students can communicate with peers or even teachers.

Furthermore, the collaboration will be nurtured in metaverse learning.

Content Will Have Immense Value.

A traditional pen and paper method was not tailored. Rather, it has a fixed content and pace of learning. However, with the metaverse, educators can create tailored content for students.

Also, it will be easy to modify the learning content for educators to achieve the best results.

It Can Bridge Traditional Nursing Training and Real-life Training.

The metaverse can be a boon to nursing training. It is one of the critical areas in a patient's healthcare. Not only learning but having a practical or hands-on experience in nursing is vital.

When the metaverse enters the nursing training, they will get realistic, practical experience. Therefore, nurses can easily apply it to actual patient care. 

Add Ons

When students learn on video conferences, the content shared in that meeting is lost immediately after the session ends. However, in the metaverse world, it will be available on the platform.

Moreover, AR and VR are already used in offering training on surgery to learners. The metaverse can enhance this experience with real-time data and environment. 

Role of iXR Labs in enhancing medical education

iXR Labs has been catering to the medical education domain with the capability of AR and Virtual Reality Labs for medical science will enhance the learning ability and experience of students.

A thorough and advanced take on academic content will be helpful for medical students. Along with medical sciences, AR and VR-based solutions for engineering and science learning.

Conclusion

The metaverse is emerging and advanced technology. It has the potential to benefit from augmented learning in medical education on many levels, such as content, social interaction, immersive learning experience, practical-oriented education, and a robust environment.

This will nourish learning and can have a holistic impact on students. It is not so far when we will witness the boom of the metaverse education platform for medical education.
